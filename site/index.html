USD/TRY — Crawl Path Regression

The CBRT lets the lira float inside a managed path. A straight line through log price is a constant % crawl, so the fitted slope is the pace and the residuals are the float around it.

Method & caveats
  • Why logs. A managed crawl is a constant percentage pace. In logs that is a straight line, so OLS on log(rate) recovers the pace directly and the residuals are (very nearly) percent deviations.
  • History starts in July 2023, the first full month after the June 2023 policy turn. The step-devaluation itself is excluded on purpose: a single +24% month widens every fitted band and swamps the shared y-axis on the faceted panels. Everything here therefore describes the managed-crawl era and says nothing about what came before it.
  • Annualisation uses 252 trading sessions: (exp(slope × 252) − 1) × 100.
  • Bands are ±1σ/±2σ of the in-sample residuals, not an announced corridor. The CBRT publishes no explicit band; this is the corridor implied by how the rate has actually behaved.
  • Breakpoints come from binary segmentation with a BIC stopping rule. They are descriptive — they do not identify policy decisions, and a break can reflect market stress rather than a change in the managed pace.
  • The estimator is a conditional distribution, not a forecast. It answers "where does the rate sit if this crawl keeps running", modelling the deviation as AR(1) around the fitted line and adding the uncertainty in the slope itself (with the effective sample size cut for autocorrelation). What it does not and cannot price is the chance the path is repriced — which, over any horizon worth asking about, is the risk that dominates. A ±1.5% one-year band for USD/TRY is only sane read as "assuming nothing changes"; it would be indefensible as an unconditional forecast. Holidays are not modelled, so a horizon spanning one is a session or two long.
  • Weekday-by-regime is not an independent test. A week's class is set by its total move, and the weekday means are components of that same total, so the two are linked by construction. What the panel can still say is where the difference between classes sits: if Friday were driving the labels, Friday would grow with the class, and it largely does not.
  • Significance tests use a normal approximation and take each session as independent. With residual autocorrelation present, small weekday p-values should be read as suggestive rather than conclusive.
  • R² is not evidence of management on its own — any smooth trending series scores high. The unusually small residual σ next to the trend is the informative part.
  • Data is the daily market close, not the CBRT official fixing. Set EVDS_API_KEY before running the fetcher to add the official rate.
